👨‍💻 About Me

I am an Informatics Engineer, Lecturer, and Researcher working at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Internet of Things, Embedded Systems, Data Analytics, and Intelligent Decision Systems.

My research and engineering activities focus on transforming real-world problems into measurable, data-driven, and intelligent technology solutions.

I am particularly interested in developing systems that combine:

«Sensors → IoT → Data → AI/ML → Intelligent Decision → Real-World Action»

My approach is not limited to developing software or hardware independently, but focuses on building end-to-end intelligent systems that can acquire data, process information, make decisions, and perform adaptive actions.

💧 Smart Water Tank — Fuzzy Sugeno

Adaptive Pump Control Using IoT & Fuzzy Inference

An IoT-based smart water tank system designed to investigate adaptive control of a water distribution pump using the Fuzzy Sugeno method.

Control Architecture

             Water Level
                  │
                  ▼
              ┌───────┐
              │       │
              │ Fuzzy │
Water Flow ──►│Sugeno │
              │       │
              └───┬───┘
                  │
                  ▼
          Pump Operating Time
                  │
                  ▼
          Water Distribution

Research Variables

Inputs

- Water level
- Water flow rate

Output

- Distribution pump operating duration

Evaluation

The adaptive fuzzy controller is evaluated against conventional ON/OFF control based on:

- Pump operating time
- Control responsiveness
- Water distribution
- Estimated energy consumption

Technology: "ESP32" · "IoT" · "Fuzzy Sugeno" · "Flow Sensor" · "Water Level Sensor"
